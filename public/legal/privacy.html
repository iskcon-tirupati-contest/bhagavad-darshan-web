Privacy Policy — Bhagavad Darshan

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Bhagavad Darshan (ISKCON Tirupati) provides magazine subscription services through our Android app.

Data we collect

  • Name, phone number, and delivery address
  • Subscription and payment references (via Razorpay / UPI)
  • App login via phone number and WhatsApp OTP
  • Optional payment-proof photos when a payment needs verification

How we use data

  • Create and manage magazine subscriptions
  • Process payments and renewals
  • Deliver magazines and send service messages
  • Customer support

Sharing

We may share necessary data with payment processors (for example Razorpay) and delivery partners. We do not sell personal data.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS where available. Access is limited to authorised temple staff.
