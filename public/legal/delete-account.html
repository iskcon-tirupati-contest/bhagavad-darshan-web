You can request deletion of your Bhagavad Darshan app account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email or WhatsApp message from the phone number registered in the app.
  2. Write: Please delete my Bhagavad Darshan account.
  3. Include your full name and 10-digit mobile number.

What happens next

We verify that the request comes from the account owner and delete your account data within 30 days.
